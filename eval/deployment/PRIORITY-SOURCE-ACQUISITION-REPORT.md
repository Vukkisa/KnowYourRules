## 3. Source 1: rule 167A

**What the notification does.** G.S.R. 575(E) (F1), made under s.136A read with s.137 of the Motor Vehicles Act, after draft G.S.R. 136(E) of 25-02-2021, inserts rule 167A "Electronic Monitoring and Enforcement of Road Safety" after rule 167. In outline (text read in full):
- **(1)** a device used for a challan needs an approval certificate signed by a designated State authority, renewed yearly; the Explanation lists speed camera, CCTV, speed gun, body-worn camera, dashboard camera, ANPR and weigh-in machine, and other technology the State specifies.
- **(2)** States "shall ensure" devices at high-risk corridors on NH and SH and at critical junctions in cities of over one million, including a table of 132 cities. **Telangana entries: Hyderabad, Nalgonda, Patancheruvu, Sangareddy** (serials 101 to 104).
- **(3)** the offences for which footage can support a challan: speed (ss.112, 183), parking (s.122), s.128, helmets (s.129), s.184 acts including red-light jumping and hand-held devices, overweight (s.194(1)), seat belts (s.194B), contravention of regulations 6 and 36 of the **Motor Vehicles (Driving) Regulations, 2017** (s.177A), goods carriage carrying passengers (s.66), projecting loads (s.194(1A)), emergency vehicles (s.194E).
- **(4) to (10)** signage, body cameras, challan contents (including an Evidence Act s.65B(4) certificate), challan in the registered owner's name, payment, notice within 15 days, owner's right to show he was not driving.

**Later amendment: G.S.R. 48(E) (F2).** It substitutes rule 167 ("Procedure for issuance and payment of challan", 13 sub-rules) and, in clause 4, amends rule 167A:
- **(7)** substituted: challan delivered by hand or physical means within 15 days, or by electronic means within 3 days; a proviso for offline recording where connectivity fails.
- **(8)(a)** cross-reference changed from "sub-rules (5), (6) and (7)" to "sub-rules (5) to (11)" of rule 167.
- **(9)** words inserted on the 3-day electronic and physical time limits.
- **(10)** the owner's claim of non-driving now goes "including on the portal, before the specified authority under sub-rule (5) of rule 167".
- Clause 2 also inserts a new clause "(25)" in rule 21(1) (disqualification ground: five or more offences in a year from 01-01-2026).

**Comparison with existing consolidated documents.**
- The 332-page India Code PDF has **no rule 167A**, and its rule 167 is the pre-2026 text. Neither 575(E) nor 48(E) is reflected in it. (Rule 167A's absence was recorded earlier by text search; confirmed again here.)
- The Telangana G.O.Ms.No.25/2026 and 24/2026 (on the Telangana register) cite rule 167 "as amended vide G.S.R. 48(E) dated 20th January, 2026", which matches F2. G.O.Ms.No.32/2026 relies on rule 167A(1), which matches F1.

**Unresolved.**
- **Footnote conflict in F1.** The English footnote says the rules were "last amended vide G.S.R. 525(E) dated 2nd August 2021"; the Hindi footnote in the same Gazette names "575(E)" (the notification itself) with the same date. The English reading is the plausible one, but **G.S.R. 525(E) was not obtained**.
- **Rule 21 numbering.** The 332-page PDF's rule 21 already lists a clause "(25) Using mobile phone while driving a vehicle". F2 inserts another new "clause (25)" after clause (24). The collision is recorded, not interpreted. Counsel needs the current consolidated rule 21.
- **Amendments to 167A between 2021 and 2025:** not systematically checked. Within 2026, the eight final notifications I hold (07(E), 28(E), 48(E), 112(E), 298(E), 540(E), 911(E), 973(E)) were checked for the rules they amend (for 112(E), only its rule 115 clauses were seen); only 48(E) touches 167A. The remaining unidentified 2026 amendments could.
- **Draft 723(E)** (29-09-2025) not obtained. It is a draft and superseded by F2.

## 4. Source 2: Rules of the Road Regulations

**Supersession: VERIFIED.** G.S.R. 634(E) (F3) says it is made under section 118 "and in supersession of the Rules of the Road Regulations, 1989, except as respects things done or omitted to be done before such supersession", and takes effect on publication. It is titled the **Motor Vehicles (Driving) Regulations, 2017** and has at least 39 regulations (I read regulation headings up to 39; the total was not counted).

**What the 332-page CMVR PDF holds.** An appendix titled "Rules of the Road Regulations, 1989" with 33 regulations, for example reg. 22 (traffic signs and police), reg. 27 (speed), reg. 32 (production of documents). That is the **superseded** text. The 1989 original (S.O. 439(E), 12-06-1989, per a secondary source) was **not** obtained.

**Where the earlier map points to the wrong text.** `PRIORITY-15-QUESTION-LEGAL-SOURCE-MAP.md` cites "reg 22", "reg 27" and "reg 32" of the 1989 text for Q5, Q6, Q8. The operative instrument is the 2017 one:
- red traffic light: **reg. 12(1)** (stop before the stop line); traffic control signals generally reg. 12
- speed: **reg. 16**; lane traffic: reg. 6; overtaking: reg. 14
- stopping and parking: reg. 22; horns and silence zones: reg. 23
- registration plates: reg. 36
- mobile phones: **reg. 37** ("shall not use any hand held mobile phone or other communication device")
- production of documents: **reg. 38**. Text as made: transport vehicles carry originals; non-transport vehicles carry the driving licence and PUC certificate, and RC and insurance "or photocopies thereof"; production on demand; 15 days for documents not in hand.

**Later amendments found (final).**
- **G.S.R. 586(E), 25-09-2020, from 01-10-2020 (F4):** reg. 37 gets a proviso (hand-held device allowed solely for route navigation, fixed on the dashboard); reg. 38 changed: "documents in physical or electronic form"; new reg. 38(5) (after inspecting valid electronic documents, physical forms shall not be demanded).
- **G.S.R. 38(E), 15-01-2024 (F5), clause 12:** "rear view mirror" replaced by "rear view mirror or devices for indirect vision" wherever it occurs in the 2017 Regulations. (Some secondary sites describe 38(E) as amending regulations 8(1)(g), 8(3)(b) and 15(4); the Gazette text does not say so. Treat that claim as wrong until shown otherwise.)
- Rule 167A(3) (F1) already uses regs 6 and 36 of the 2017 Regulations, indicating the 2017 text was the one in use in 2021.

**Not found.** No replacement or amendment of the 2017 Regulations after 2024 turned up in the Gazette texts I hold, in MoRTH's notification list, or in one web search. That is absence from limited searching, not proof. **Whether the 2017 Regulations stand current at 16-11-2026: PARTIALLY VERIFIED** (two amendments obtained; later changes not excluded).

**Comparison note.** The 2017 text of reg. 38 and rule 139 of the CMVR (production of documents) differ in wording and scope; how they interact is a legal question and is not addressed here.

## 6. Source 4: central child-safety provisions (s.129)

**The Act.** The Motor Vehicles (Amendment) Act, 2019 (F6), s.44, substitutes s.129. As substituted: every person above four years of age driving, riding or carried on a motorcycle must wear protective headgear conforming to standards prescribed by the Central Government (turban proviso for Sikhs), and a **second proviso**: "the Central Government may by rules provide for measures for the safety of children below four years of age riding or being carried on a motorcycle." The s.129 text in the 15-08-2026 India Code consolidation (176-page PDF) matches the Gazette text, and its footnote gives "w.e.f. 15-02-2022".

**Commencement: VERIFIED.** S.O. 691(E) (F7), 15-02-2022: "appoints the 15th day of February, 2022 as the date on which section 44 of the said Act shall come into force." This matches the India Code footnote. (Section 44 is not in the earlier commencement notifications: S.O. 3110(E) of 28-08-2019 covers sections 2 to 43 and others but skips 44; S.O. 3311(E) of 25-09-2020 lists 45, 74, 88, 90, 91; S.O. 859(E) of 25-02-2022 lists 50 to 57 and 93. All three were opened and checked.)

**The rule: G.S.R. 126(E), 15-02-2022 (F8), Central Motor Vehicles (Second Amendment) Rules, 2022.** Made under clause (aa) of s.137 after draft G.S.R. 758(E) of 21-10-2021 (F9). Inserts **rule 138(7)**. Text: after one year from the date of publication of the Rules, the driver of a motorcycle shall ensure, when carrying a child **between nine months and four years** as a pillion: (i) a safety harness attaching the child to the driver (light, adjustable, waterproof, durable; nylon with cushioning; designed for up to 30 kg); (ii) the child wears a crash helmet or bicycle helmet complying with ASTM 1447 / BS EN 1080 / BS EN 1078 until the Bureau of Indian Standards prescribes specifications; (iii) speed **not more than 40 kmph** with a child up to four years. The date from which the duties bite is for counsel to compute from "after one year from the date of publication"; I have not interpreted it.

**Draft versus final.** Draft 758(E) is a draft and has been finalised by 126(E). Both texts are held. The draft's wording on one year, harness, crash helmet and 40 kmph matches the final on a skim; I did not diff every word.

**Comparison with existing consolidated documents.**
- The 332-page CMVR PDF's rule 138 ends at sub-rule (6) (child up to twelve in a car, M1 vehicles made on or after 01-10-2014). **Rule 138(7) is absent.** This closes gap A3 of the gap list as an *acquisition*, not as a change to the consolidation.
- The Act's s.129 in the 176-page PDF matches the Gazette text of s.44.
- A separate earlier conflict stands: Act s.194B(2) speaks of children under 14 in cars, rule 138(6) of children up to 12. Unchanged.
- The earlier note that the 2019 Amendment Act was dated 09-08-2019 or 30-08-2019 is settled by F6: the Gazette gives **assent on 9 August 2019** (Part II s.1, No. 51). The 30-08-2019 date came from the India Code record and is not the Gazette date.

**Not found.** No later amendment to rule 138(7) turned up in what I read. Not systematically checked. G.S.R. 07(E) of 2026 amends rule 138(4) only.
