## 7b. Why ingestion was not done

1. The evidence is not sufficient (section 7a): no current text exists for the rules that answer the questions.
2. The pipeline cannot hold Central documents without changing code: the manifest validator requires `jurisdiction: "Telangana"` for the manifest and for every document (`backend/tg_manifest.py`), and retrieval is "always filtered to state=Telangana, never Central or other states" (`backend/server.py`). Labelling a Central Act as Telangana would falsify its provenance, and supporting Central documents is an architecture change that was out of scope.
3. Any ingested document would stay `pending` in the lifecycle registry and be filtered out until a signed approval exists, so ingestion would not change what the app can answer.
