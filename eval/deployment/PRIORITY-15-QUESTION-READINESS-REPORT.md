### How the three statuses were applied

- **SOURCE READY**: every provision the core answer needs is held as an official text, technically verified, and no identified gap, conflict or unverified layer touches it. For Q4 that means the Act alone (with the Act-level caveat in section 4, item C4).
- **PARTIALLY READY**: some required sources are held and verified; a named gap, unverified layer or open conflict remains. Each is listed.
- **NOT READY**: an essential provision is missing, shown to be stale, or unresolved.
- Two rules were applied consistently. (a) A provision read **only** from P332 (stale, last dated footnote 2016) or only from the Telangana HTML snapshot (`unverified_snapshot`) cannot support SOURCE READY. (b) The Telangana compounding order (G.O.Ms.No.42/2020) is treated as needed only where the Act text held makes the offence compoundable (s.200(1)); where it does not, the order is listed as an advocate question.

### Q1. Do I have to wear a helmet on a two-wheeler in Telangana?
- **Act:** s.129 as substituted by Act 32 of 2019 s.44 (F6, 45 pages), in force 15-02-2022 (F7, S.O. 691(E)); s.128; s.137(aa). **CMVR:** 138(7) (F8, 126(E)); 138(4)(f), 123 (P332). **Regulations:** none. **Telangana:** TMVR r.437 (snapshot).
- **Currency:** s.129 and 138(7) amended-in and verified; 138(4)(f) and 123 unresolved (P332 only); TMVR r.437 unresolved.
- **Sufficient for a grounded answer?** The duty and the child-gear rule: yes at the text level. The standard the helmet must meet: no.
- **Needed before enabling (after advocate review):**
  1. The Central standard that s.129 says "may be prescribed by the Central Government" for protective headgear: a notification, rule or BIS specification. Not identified in any held document. 138(4)(f) points at BIS but is itself from P332.
  2. The BIS specifications that 126(E) defers to "till such time" they are prescribed (safety harness; child helmet), if the answer states gear standards.
  3. An official Telangana text of TMVR r.437 (Telangana Gazette or an official consolidation) replacing the snapshot.
  4. Advocate points (no document): s.129 says "above four years", 138(7) says "between nine months and four years"; the effect of the one-year deferral in 138(7).
