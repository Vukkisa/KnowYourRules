# Central Motor Vehicles Rules, 1989: acquisition and amendment-coverage report

Written 2026-11-16. Covers the search for the official CMVR text and its amendments up to 16-11-2026. Telangana remains the primary jurisdiction; the CMVR is supporting central law.

**Acquisition and technical verification only. Nothing is ingested, registered, activated or approved. No legal approval is claimed.**

Evidence labels: **VERIFIED** (official document opened and read), **PARTIALLY VERIFIED** (official, but only part read or only cited by another official document), **UNVERIFIED** (reported but no official source found), **UNRESOLVED** (cannot be settled from the evidence).

## 4. The principal notification

- **G.S.R. 590(E), dated 2 June 1989**, Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (i). Evidence: the "Note" at the foot of each 2026 amending notice says so, for example G.S.R. 540(E), G.S.R. 973(E) and G.S.R. 07(E) (VERIFIED as a citation), and a footnote in the India Code files repeats it.
- **The original Gazette PDF was not found** on e-Gazette (the portal's coverage of 1989 is not available through the paths I used), MoRTH or India Code. Status: **UNVERIFIED** as a document. Source needed: the Department of Publication's archive or the Legislative Department.
- The rules came into force on 1 July 1989 (rule 1(2), per the India Code text).

## 7. Gaps, conflicts and unresolved points

1. **No official, dated, current consolidation exists in what I could reach.** **UNRESOLVED** whether the Ministry or the Legislative Department keeps one off these sites.
2. **Principal Gazette (G.S.R. 590(E)) not obtained.** UNVERIFIED as a document.
3. **At least 8 of the 15 "2026 Amendment Rules" are unidentified** (only the 1st, 5th, 10th and 15th are named in the Gazette text I read; 911(E) is unnamed). The e-Gazette sweep was partial.
4. **Amendments 2017–2025:** not reconstructed. All are unincorporated in the available consolidations.
5. **Date conflicts:** G.S.R. 540(E) (notified 19 June, Gazette 1 July); other notices differ between notification date and Gazette issue date by one to four days. The e-Gazette "publish date" can be later still (973(E): 5 Nov issue, 10 Nov publish).
6. **MoRTH's list is incomplete and has errors** (see the Motor Vehicles Act report, section 9.2, for another wrong link).
7. **Commencement dates that differ from publication:** G.S.R. 07(E) (AVAS from 01-10-2026 and 01-10-2027), G.S.R. 540(E) (from 01-10-2027). G.S.R. 911(E) and 973(E): on publication; the amended tables may have their own phased dates inside the text, which I did not fully read.
8. **Corrigenda:** not searched.

## 8. Not done (scope)

## 9. Currency statement

The Central Motor Vehicles Rules, 1989 **cannot reasonably be described as current through 16 November 2026** on the evidence acquired. The only official-portal copies are old; five final 2026 amendments that I opened are not in them; at least eight more 2026 amendment Rules are unidentified.
