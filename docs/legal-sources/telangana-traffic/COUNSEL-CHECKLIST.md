## A. Documents to confirm once retrieved
- [ ] **TG-TMVR:** the operative text of the Telangana Motor Vehicles Rules, 1989, including the Rule 81 amendment by G.O.Ms.No. 77 of 15-11-2025 (final; the 13-08-2025 preliminary notification is G.O.Ms.No. 54).
- [ ] **TG-TMVTA, TG-A8-2026, TG-GO10-2026:** the Taxation Act, 1963 (Act 5 of 1963), the Amendment Act No. 8 of 2026, and its commencement date.
- [ ] **TG-GO53-2025, TG-GO16-2026:** the Third, Sixth and Seventh Schedule amendments (life tax).
- [ ] **TG-TMVTR:** the Taxation Rules, 1963, and the instrument adapting them to Telangana.
- [ ] **TG-COMP-\*:** which compounding schedule is in force: the 2006 G.O.s, G.O.Ms.No. 332 of 2008, or G.O.Ms.No. 42 of 2020.
- [ ] **TG-GO24/25/32-2026:** the 2026 e-challan and electronic enforcement orders.

## B. Decisions to record in Section 5
1. **Pre-2014 orders.** Do Andhra Pradesh-era G.O.s (2006, 2008) apply in Telangana, and under which adaptation or continuation instrument?
2. **Compounding schedule.** Can a verified older schedule be used while G.O.Ms.No. 42 of 2020 remains unresolved? If so, how should that uncertainty be shown to users?
3. **2026 cess.** Is the 2026 "Road Safety Cess" in the supplied summary actually what Act No. 8 of 2026 and G.O.Ms.No. 16 of 2026 contain? What vehicle classes, rates and exemptions apply?
4. **Penalty points.** Is the penalty-point system (G.O.Ms.No. 26) operative, and where?
5. **Case law.** Should High Court decisions be included, and how should they be distinguished from statutes?
6. **Adapted texts.** Are HTML texts on the Transport Department site acceptable as the source text, or are gazette or official PDF texts required?
7. **Final set.** What is the exact approved document set, version, jurisdiction, exclusions and waivers?
