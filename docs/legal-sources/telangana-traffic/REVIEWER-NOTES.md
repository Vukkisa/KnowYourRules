# Telangana reviewer notes

**Prepared:** 29 October 2026. These are research notes for the reviewer, not legal advice. Nothing here verifies that any instrument is in force.

## What was checked, and how

**Supplied summary.** I read all 4 pages. It reports some source sets as "primary-source verified", but it also says it "does not independently authenticate repository files". The evidence those statements rest on (the original handoff and the archived PDFs) is **not present**, so its "verified" labels are **not adopted** here. Every status in this package starts from "not retrieved".

**Telangana Transport Department website.** The Acts & Rules pages list:
- TG Motor Vehicles Taxation Act, 1963
- TG Motor Vehicles Taxation Rules, 1963
- TG Motor Vehicles Rules, 1989
- several Central Acts and Rules

The Rules are published as HTML. Notably:
- The Taxation Rules page carries an Andhra Pradesh title and header, while rule 1 of its text names the Telangana Rules and Telangana's territory.
- TMVR Chapter 1's page title reads "Andhra Pradesh Control of Transport Vehicles".

This is consistent with adapted texts. The adaptation instruments themselves were not seen.

**Telangana G.O. register (GOIR).** I filtered to Transport Roads and Building, MS, for 2025 and 2026. Rows with Download/View links:

| Year | Instruments |
|---|---|
| 2025 | G.O.Ms.No. 51, 53, 54, 60 and 77 |
| 2026 | G.O.Ms.No. 10, 16, 24, 25, 32, 48 and 50 |

Subjects are quoted in the source manifest. A search for G.O.Ms.No. 42 of 2020 (TRB, MS) returned **"No Records Found."**

## Differences from the supplied summary
1. **Compounding:** the Transport Department site links a 2008 compounding order (`2008trb_ms332_compounding.pdf`). The summary does not mention it; it cites only the 2006 G.O.s 54 and 242 and the 2020 G.O.Ms.No. 42.
2. **2026 tax instruments:** GOIR shows Act No. 8 of 2026, commenced by G.O.Ms.No. 10 of 25-02-2026, and a further Seventh Schedule amendment, G.O.Ms.No. 16 of 20-03-2026. The summary describes a "Road Safety Cess" and a 7.5% provision; neither appears in the GOIR subjects.
3. **2026 enforcement orders:** G.O.Ms.No. 24, 25 and 32 of 2026, on challans and electronic enforcement under CMVR Rules 167 and 167A, are not in the summary.
4. **The nine archived PDFs** and their hashes are unavailable for comparison.

## Questions of applicability (for counsel)
- **Pre-2014 orders.** G.O.s from 2006 and 2008 come from the combined Andhra Pradesh government. Whether each continues in Telangana depends on the Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act, 2014, the Telangana adaptation orders, and any later Telangana instrument. **None of these has been examined.**
- **Adapted texts.** The HTML texts show adaptation but carry no "as amended up to" date.

## Technical note (not legal)
The production index is a **single Chroma collection for all states**, and the build requires every configured file (Option A in `../../../archive/abandoned-gujarat/docs/legal-source-remediation.md`). That includes the blocked Gujarat files. A "Telangana-only production index" would mean one of:
- (a) a separate collection or index directory and server configuration, or
- (b) rebuilding the shared index without the other states.

Option (b) would remove the existing coverage. Either is a product decision to make after legal approval. No index was built or changed.
