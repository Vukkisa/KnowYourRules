# Telangana legal-source review handoff

**Prepared:** 29 October 2026 · **Gate: HOLD INDEX BUILD.** This document is for counsel. It is not legal advice and contains no approval.

## 1. Scope
Sources the KnowYourRules chatbot would use for Telangana questions:
- the Telangana Motor Vehicles Rules, 1989
- the Telangana Motor Vehicles Taxation Act, 1963 and Taxation Rules, 1963, with their 2025–26 amendments
- compounding and enforcement orders

**Scope decision (user, 08-11-2026): Telangana only.** *(Historical; superseded by the 15-11-2026 note below.)* Only Telangana state instruments are to be indexed. Central texts are **excluded**: the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988, the Central Motor Vehicles Rules, 1989 and the Rules of the Road Regulations, 1989.

**Scope decision note (project owner, 15-11-2026):** Updated project scope: KnowYourRules covers Telangana traffic rules together with the relevant central legislation they depend on. Telangana remains the primary jurisdiction; applicable central legislation and regulations are supporting legal sources. This is not an all-India traffic-law product. This is the project owner's scope decision. It is not legal counsel approval, and no source is approved by it.

**Consequence for counsel:** offences and maximum penalties are defined in the Central Act, so answers on traffic fines will rest only on Telangana's own instruments, such as the compounding schedule under s.200. That makes the missing s.200 schedule (G7) essential.

Gujarat is out of scope and is handled separately in `../`.
