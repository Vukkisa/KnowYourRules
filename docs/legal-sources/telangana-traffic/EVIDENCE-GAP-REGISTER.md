A **missing file** gap means no official copy is stored in this package. A **missing verification** gap means a document is listed or held, but its content or legal status has not been confirmed. **Blocking** means production indexing cannot proceed until the gap is resolved, or qualified counsel waives it in writing in the approval block of [`LEGAL-REVIEW-HANDOFF.md`](LEGAL-REVIEW-HANDOFF.md).
